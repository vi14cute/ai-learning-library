## How these fit ClearStep

Start building the lightweight checklist before completing entire ML courses. Use Microsoft ML lessons for foundations, algorithms/PyTorch resources for selected concepts, and MLOps when evaluation/deployment becomes relevant. Training a model is not required for the initial app. Forks remain linked to their authors and do not install software on the laptop.
